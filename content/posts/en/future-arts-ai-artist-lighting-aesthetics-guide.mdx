Use AI image tools long enough and eventually you hit a wall. However long the prompt, results look alike, and AI often simply ignores modifiers such as “beautiful” or “like a movie.”

The key past that wall is usually just one term: **the precise name of a style.**

“A woman in a forest” and “a woman in a forest, Alphonse Mucha style, golden-hour lighting, Art Nouveau aesthetics” produce entirely different results. The moment you add a painter, lighting technique, or art movement, AI draws on hundreds of thousands of references and converges in that direction.

I have organized **150 style modifiers** whose effects I have tested in actual use into three categories: artists, lighting, and aesthetic movements.

## Part 2. Lighting styles — 40 types

Among single variables, lighting changes an image’s mood most strongly. The same subject can evoke an entirely different emotion through lighting alone.

### Natural light

→ **Golden hour** — Warm amber tones, long shadows, cinematic warmth

→ **Blue hour** — Cool twilight, soft violet skies, quiet atmosphere

→ **Magic hour** — Just after sunrise or just before sunset

→ **Overcast diffused light** — Even and shadowless, muted and flat

→ **Harsh midday sun** — High contrast, black shadows, bleached highlights

→ **Dappled light** — Sunlight filtering through leaves, spotted patterns, dreaminess

→ **Backlit / contre-jour** — A silhouetted subject or a translucent glow

→ **Rainy day light** — Muted gray, reflective puddles, introspection

> “The Secret Garden pond at Changdeokgung Palace, golden hour lighting, reflected autumn leaves, film grain”

![AI image in a natural-light style](/images/posts/14/Secret_garden_pond_202603260908.webp)

### Studio and artificial light

→ **Chiaroscuro** — Extreme light-dark contrast, Renaissance drama

→ **Rembrandt lighting** — A triangular highlight on one side of the face, shadow on the other

→ **Rim lighting** — A contour of light wrapping the subject’s edge

→ **Neon lighting** — Electric-colored glow, cyberpunk atmosphere, pink, blue, and green

→ **Bioluminescent lighting** — Organic luminous elements, underwater fantasy

→ **Candlelight** — Warm flicker, intimacy, old-world atmosphere

→ **Firelight** — Orange warmth, dancing light, primal feeling

→ **High key lighting** — Very bright with minimal shadows, light and clean

→ **Low key lighting** — Predominantly dark with selective highlights, dramatic, noir

## The style-combination formula

The real magic happens when several modifiers are layered.

**Basic formula:**

```
[Subject] + [Artist style] + [Lighting] + [Aesthetics] + [Medium]
```

**Four Korean-context examples:**

**1. A lonely Seoul dawn**
```
An office worker sitting alone outside a convenience store in Gangnam at 3 a.m., Edward Hopper style,
fluorescent low key lighting, urban loneliness aesthetic,
Cinestill 800T film, digital painting
```

**2. Bugaksan fantasy**
```
A woman standing atop Bugaksan, Yoshitaka Amano style,
bioluminescent lighting, Studio Ghibli aesthetics,
watercolor illustration, moonlight glowing
```

**3. Hongdae cyberpunk**
```
A Hongdae alley on a rainy night, Moebius clean line art style,
neon lighting + volumetric light, cyberpunk aesthetic,
mixed-media ink and watercolor
```

**4. Gyeongbokgung Art Nouveau**
```
A woman in hanbok before Gwanghwamun Gate at Gyeongbokgung Palace, Alphonse Mucha style,
golden hour lighting, Art Nouveau aesthetics,
ornamental borders and gold-leaf details, digital painting
```

## Tips for using prompt modifiers

**Start with an artist.** An artist’s name carries more visual information per word than any other modifier. Lighting then refines the mood on top of it.

**Less is better.** Two or three modifiers often work better than ten. AI needs room to interpret.

**Specificity beats vagueness.** “Rembrandt lighting” is better than “dramatic lighting,” and “Ukiyo-e” is far more precise than “Japanese style.”

**Test an aesthetic on its own first.** Enter only “vaporwave aesthetic” or “dark academia,” without a subject, and see what AI produces by default. Layering the subject afterward is much more stable.

**Try unexpected combinations.** Seemingly incompatible pairings such as “Baroque composition, cyberpunk lighting” or “cottagecore aesthetics, sumi-e style” can produce more original results.

**Always specify the medium.** Oil painting, watercolor, digital art, photography: that one term determines the entire texture of the output.

---

Learning the language of style is like learning a new language. It feels unfamiliar at first, but a few key terms completely change the quality of the conversation with AI. Pick the painter’s name that attracts you most today and add it to a prompt.
