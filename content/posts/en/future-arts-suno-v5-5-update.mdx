On March 26, 2026, Suno unveiled v5.5.

Honestly, I paused when I saw the announcement. *Making a song in my own voice.* That had become possible. AI music tools had continued to improve in quality, but a sense of 'me' was always missing. v5.5 tackles that directly.

This is the slogan Suno chose for the update.

> **"More Expressive. More You."**
> *With greater expression. More like yourself.*

One sentence CEO Mikey Shulman posted on the blog also stood out: "The best music starts with a human." The human is the starting point, rather than AI taking center stage.

---

## The Three Core Features of v5.5

The keyword in this update is **personalization**, more than a leap in technical performance. All three new features point in the same direction.

---

## 1. Voices — Singing in Your Own Voice

![Suno v5.5 Voices feature](/images/posts/suno-v5-5-voices.webp)

> *"The human voice is the oldest instrument we have."*
> — Suno official blog

This feature is **exclusive to Pro/Premier**.

Suno went as far as calling it "the community’s most requested feature." The name is simple, but the substance runs deep.

Upload audio of yourself singing or record it live, and Suno learns your vocal characteristics to apply to subsequently generated tracks. Your voice becomes an instrument.

**The safeguards against misuse are carefully designed too.** It matches the uploaded voice against a liveness check in which you speak a random sentence, confirming it really is your voice. Created voices are **private** by default, so other people cannot use yours without permission. Voice sharing is planned for a later update.

People who have used it say, "The voice doesn’t sit on top of the track; it sings with the track." That seems to capture the point.

## 2. Custom Models — Your Own Sonic DNA

![Suno Custom Models](/images/posts/suno-v5-5-custom-model.webp)

**Exclusive to Pro/Premier; create up to 3**

Upload music you have made to Suno, and it creates **your own model** trained on that style.

If, for example, you have been making lofi, acoustic indie or dreamy synth-pop tracks, a custom model learns that style. Future prompts then produce results that start from 'your style.'

This is a powerful feature for artists and producers who need a **consistent musical identity**. It is a problem when every song on an album has a different atmosphere. A custom model can hold that consistency together.

---

## 3. My Taste — AI That Quietly Gets to Know You

![Suno My Taste personalization](/images/posts/suno-v5-5-my-taste.webp)

**Available to all users, including the free plan**

This is the quietest feature, but also the most widely applicable.

Suno automatically learns your preferred genres, moods, tempos and styles over time. No separate setup is needed. Like Spotify’s algorithm, it adapts to your tastes the more you use it.

It seems particularly useful for people who struggle with prompts or do not want to explain everything at length each time. At some point, you may find yourself asking, “Why have the results been matching my style so well lately?”

---

## Chat Mode — Making Music Through Conversation

**Chat Mode**, also enhanced alongside v5.5, deserves attention.

It lets you make music conversationally in natural language instead of writing technical prompts. Enter something like "a late-night feeling, a little sad and warm," and Suno converts it into parameters.

But experts make one central point: **Use Chat Mode and Prompt Mode together.** Chat Mode for exploring ideas, Prompt Mode for precise control. They complement each other rather than one replacing the other.

---

## How Much Has Audio Quality Changed?

The jump from 24kHz to **44.1kHz (CD quality)** in v5 was already substantial, and v5.5 refines the detail further.

- Vocals: Much more natural breathing, vibrato and dynamic expression
- Instruments: Improved guitar-string resonance, piano sustain and drum punch
- Mix: "Radio-ready" level balance
- Processing speed: Maintains a speed 10 times faster than v5

There is still feedback that heavy electric guitar tones and extreme genre crossovers can produce messy results. It is not perfect, but the direction is clear.

## The Competitive Landscape: Released on the Same Day as Google Lyria 3 Pro

Interestingly, v5.5 launched on the same day as Google’s Lyria 3 Pro announcement, March 26. It seems unlikely to be a coincidence.

Competition in AI music generation is heating up in earnest. Suno has partnered with Warner Music Group and announced plans to launch a **next-generation model** developed with the music industry later this year. v5.5 lays the groundwork.
