After using AI image tools for a while, you eventually hit a wall: the results stop improving beyond "fairly decent." There is usually one reason: **your style vocabulary is too limited.**

Vague instructions such as "make it cinematic," "make it beautiful" or "give it feeling" let AI retreat to a safe average. Specify a style name, film stock, shooting technique or art movement, however, and the result changes completely.

This is my collection of **more than 150 style prompts across 10 categories**, developed through hundreds of rounds of trial and error. They are ready to use in all the major image AIs, including ChatGPT, Midjourney and Nano Banana 2. I selected only prompts I have actually tested with Korean subjects and locations.

---

## The Four Layers of a Good Prompt

Before choosing a style, keep this structure in mind. The most successful prompts invariably contain these four layers.

1. **Output medium or render format** — surreal photography, digital painting, 3D render, concept art
2. **Style name or visual movement** — Unreal Engine 5 photorealism, solarpunk matte painting
3. **Lighting direction and emotional mood** — volumetric god rays, cold clinical lighting, golden backlighting, bioluminescent glow
4. **Aspect ratio and dimensions** — 16:9, 1:1, 2.39:1 CinemaScope

The key is to combine styles. The most original results emerge where two styles intersect.

## Finally: Mixing Styles Matters More Than Simply Using Them

These more than 150 styles are a **palette**, not a catalog. Using one at a time is fine, but truly original results emerge when two or more styles collide.

Vermeer lighting + a solarpunk setting. Hasselblad photorealism + Hokusai waves. WPA murals + RTX cyberpunk.

Where familiar and unfamiliar visual grammars meet, a scene no one has seen before can emerge. Finding that intersection is the real pleasure of prompt design.
