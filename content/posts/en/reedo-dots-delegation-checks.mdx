If you have created two AI employees but still decide who should do what first every time, you are still carrying the team's workflow yourself. This video shows what happened when the creator gave dots, acting as team leader, the assignments, tasks, and prohibited actions together and received files organized by date.

Corner Coffee and its menu are fictional settings for teaching materials. Although the video was published on October 15, 2026, the screens showing task delegation and file saving come from an actual demonstration on October 6. Human decisions were still needed after the one-line delegation, and the exercise did not verify the quality of the completed files or any increase in audience response.

**Delegate the work in one request, then finish with three checks: files for each date, prohibited actions, and a direct reading of the content.**

## What went into that one-line request?

The first request asked the growth strategist to review the records in inputs and outline the direction for October 9 and 10, then have the content writer create two days of content sets based on that direction. It also stated that this was a fictional-brand exercise, prohibited image generation and social media scheduling, and requested only the locations of the final files.

The benefit of a single request here is not simply its brevity, but that it lays out the connections between tasks at once. It puts strategy before writing and combines prohibited actions with the required completion-report format. This shifts the workflow from repeatedly instructing individual employees to entrusting the sequence to a team leader.

dots replied that it would split the work between the strategist and then the writer, but the first result was not a finished version. Although the strategy was complete, the menu and shooting scene for October 9 had not been decided, so the content was saved as a draft for review. This is a reminder not to assume that every decision is settled just because a file exists.

## Why did it ask the person again?

dots asked whether it could finalize the fictional menu for October 9 as a slice of lemon pound cake. The user replied that it should proceed with this fictional setting for the lesson and label it as such in the files as well. Final versions for both dates were then saved, including wording that identified the setting as fictional and user-approved, not intended for actual sales.

This is an example of AI returning a decision to the person rather than arbitrarily treating unknown operational information as fact. If you try this, it helps to specify who will approve undecided items and whether the work should remain a draft until approval. There is no guarantee that this procedure will always recur, however, so you need to check for missing information yourself in real work.

Check the returned results in three ways: whether all files for the requested dates exist, whether any prohibited actions were taken, and finally whether you have read the text yourself. In this demonstration, the confirmation that no images were generated and no posts were scheduled is based on dots' reply. It is not an independent audit of every action, so a person still needs to review the files and their contents.

## Are checks still needed when you delegate without names?

Later in the video, role-assignment guidance is added to the project's instruction file. It records which tasks should go to the strategist and writer, which should be handled with skills without agents, and whether multi-day work should proceed with the strategist before the writer. The creator then requests content sets for October 12 and 13 without naming the employees.

The reply says that two dated sets were saved and their formatting and hashtags checked, but overlap with previous posts remains unverified. The subagent panel shows one completed row from before the request and two new strategist and writer rows after it. Do not count all three completed indicators as employees newly activated for this request.

The video also presents requests from the Seo-a project's actual conversation records to revise text endings, hashtag order, and scenes with complicated finger poses. Even when roles are assigned correctly, human content review remains a separate step. The limitation still stands: the fictional-brand exercise did not verify writing quality or improvements in actual audience response.

[Watch the one-line delegation and three checks in this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q0wR2zmSl0s)

Start small with your first delegation by including who is responsible, the dates and deliverables, what not to do, and what to report upon completion. When the reply arrives, do not stop at the statement that files were saved; open and read them. The key is to distinguish reducing the effort of assigning work from handing over final judgment.
